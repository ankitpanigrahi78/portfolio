Experience

3.5 years across two organisations, all of it pointed at one thing: getting combat aircraft fuel system hardware designed, reviewed and qualified.

Aerospace Design Engineer · FCL (Fluid Controls Limited)

Dec 2025 – Present · AMCA (ADA) programme

Design and development of three fuel system LRUs: a Quick Disconnecting Coupling (QDC) for ground pressurisation of the tank, a Non-Return Valve with Thermal Relief Sub-Assembly, and the Engine Feed Line Coupling for direct fuel transfer from feeder tank to engine.

What I did

  • Took all 3 LRUs end to end: specification review, conceptual calculations, multiple concepts, final selection, assembly and sub-assembly design.
  • Material selection, weight budgeting, locking mechanisms, BOMs and assembly details.
  • Theoretical stress, spring, O-ring and flow calculations; technical compliance matrices.
  • Fault Hazard Analysis, reliability support and Acceptance Test Procedures (ATP).
  • Analysis team member for FEA, CFD, thermal and modal studies.
  • GA, detail drawings and ICDs, with 3D models in SolidWorks.
  • Completed PDR for all 3 LRUs; prototype and qualification units released for CDR.

Junior Project Engineer · CTTC (Ministry of MSME)

Apr 2023 – Nov 2025 · Deputed to ADA

3-month intensive training on aerospace manufacturing workflows, CAD to testing, then deputation to the Aeronautical Development Agency for LRU and system design.

Retractable probe, drogue and relief valves (LCA Mk2) and fuel pipeline routing

  • Prepared LRU-level requirements for the combat aircraft fuel system, aligned with ISO, MIL-STD, STANAG, CEMILAC and ARDC.
  • Compliance matrices; design to MIL-STD-810H; support for PDR and CDR and indigenous development.
  • ICDs in CATIA and Enovia with full lifecycle traceability; reports and presentations.
  • Hand-calculated stress analysis; 2D schematics in AutoCAD; assembly procedures.
  • MDI, Kit of Parts, process sheets, DMUs and BOMs.
  • Fuel pipeline routing models for Navy and Air Force directorates.

Universal test rig for valves

  • 2D/3D CAD for fuel test rigs per ASME Section VIII Div 1.
  • Prepared and ran ATPs; coordinated high/low temperature, altitude and endurance tests.
